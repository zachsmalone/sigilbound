# Sigilbound canon

Original top-down beast-bonding RPG intended for a Game Boy ROM playable in Delta.
Yami Suzuki is male, 18, summoned from Japan with Sunsi Yangkia.
Great Spirit: They cannot see your true power. Remember that when they name you weak.
King assesses Sunsi first: highest power in 500 years since Orsirus. Yami starts at level 1 in Affinity, Empathy and Resolve.
Yami earns willing bonds; Sunsi's domination and public acclaim lead to his eventual Demon King identity.
Eight regional Wardens precede the Big Four, Sunsi's top generals, then the final confrontation.
102 collectable beasts: 001 Nugwolf starter; 002-100 other ordinary beasts; 101 Aurevion and 102 Noctyra legendary. Only Nugwolf is represented by this diagnostic starter record; full roster integration remains absent.
Lady Erin is recurring healing/support. Mammoth Man is an optional comedic NPC, never mandatory story progression, who offers One Peace.
Python ROM builder is the authoritative current build path. Do not add a second RGBDS path without an explicit migration decision.
